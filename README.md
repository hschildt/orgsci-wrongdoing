# Organizational wrongdoing: five cases, five theories

A corpus of public primary documents on five cases of corporate wrongdoing, coded against five theories of organizational wrongdoing, with tables comparing the cases and theories.

**Cases:** Enron, VW Dieselgate, Ford Pinto, Purdue Pharma / OxyContin, Boeing 737 MAX. Court filings, plea agreements, congressional hearings and reports, regulator and crash-investigation reports, mostly from official US, EU and German government sources.

**Theories:** strain and performance pressure; normalization of deviance and corruption; moral disengagement and neutralization; routine activity theory and the opportunity perspective; the attention-based view of the firm.

## How it was made

1. Each theory has a coding scheme in `coding_schemes/`, with one category per `## ` heading.
2. `scripts/extract_passages.py` uses Claude (Haiku 4.5 on Amazon Bedrock) to code every document against each scheme, extracting verbatim passages. Every quote is checked against the source text, and page numbers come from where the quote is found.
3. `scripts/compare_tables.py` uses Claude to summarize the passages for each theory × case × category, then to compare cases within each theory and theories within each case. Every answer is constrained to a fixed schema and checked in Python; coverage scores and rankings are computed in Python.
