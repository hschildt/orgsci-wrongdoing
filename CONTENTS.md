# OrgSciWrongdoing: contents & gaps

84 public primary documents (~190 MB) across five cases. Collected 12 Oct 2026. Source URLs: `README_sources.md`.

## Enron/
| Folder | What's in it |
|---|---|
| `Powers_Report/` | Powers Report (Board Special Investigative Committee, 1 Feb 2002), full text (.txt from SEC 8-K) + 8-K cover |
| `Whistleblower_Watkins/` | House E&C hearing Pt 3, 14 Feb 2002, Watkins testimony (326 pp); 4 trial exhibits: Watkins emails (22 Aug, 25 Oct 2001), "Disclosure steps to rebuild investor confidence" (30 Oct 2001), her handwritten notes |
| `Congress/` | House E&C hearings Pt 1 (Powers, 86 pp) & Pt 2 (Skilling, 490 pp); Senate PSI *Role of the Board in Enron's Collapse* (2002); Senate *Financial Oversight of Enron: SEC & Private-Sector Watchdogs* (2002) |
| `DOJ_charging_docs/` | Fastow complaint affidavit + superseding information; Kopper information; Causey indictment; Skilling/Lay/Causey superseding indictment |
| `DOJ_Lay_Skilling_trial/` | Verdict slips (Lay, Skilling), DAG verdict statement, witness list; **`Trial_exhibit_index_1374_exhibits.csv`**: date, exhibit no., description, PDF link for every linked trial exhibit |
| `SEC_enforcement/` | SEC complaints v. Fastow (2002) and v. Skilling & Causey (2004), .htm |

**Missing:** Watkins' Aug 2001 memo to Lay (no official full copy; quoted at length in Pt 3 hearing) · Enron email corpus (CMU; skipped deliberately) · the 1,374 trial exhibits themselves (indexed only) · Batson bankruptcy examiner reports · 10-Ks/annual reports · Arthur Andersen trial materials

## Ford_Pinto/
| Folder | What's in it |
|---|---|

| `NHTSA/` | NHTSA press-release compilations Aug–Dec 1977, Jan–Jun 1978, Jul–Dec 1978 (scanned; Pinto releases on pp. 32, 48–49, 101 / 88, 93, 117–118, 135–140 / 34, 39, 91–92, 108, 115, 128, 134–141); NHTSA recall records incl. campaign **78V143000** (June 1978 fuel tank/filler pipe recall, ~1.4M vehicles) and all Pinto/Bobcat recalls (JSON) |

**Missing:** NHTSA Investigation Report C7-38 (May 1978; not online) · original scan of the Grush–Saunby memo (Mother Jones text version only) · *State of Indiana v. Ford* (1980 reckless homicide trial) court records · Center for Auto Safety petitions (1974/77) · FMVSS 301 rulemaking (39 FR 10588, 1974) · scholarly accounts (Gioia 1992; Lee & Ermann 1999; Schwartz 1991) pending university login
